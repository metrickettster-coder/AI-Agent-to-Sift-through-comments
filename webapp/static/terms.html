What TitleSift does

TitleSift reads what people wrote in a YouTube video's public comments and description and suggests the name of the anime, manhwa, manga, webtoon, movie or TV show shown. The answers come from other viewers and public databases, so they can be wrong or missing. Check the links we give before relying on an answer.

Other people's content

Videos, comments, titles and artwork belong to their creators and owners. TitleSift shows short quotes of public comments to explain where an answer came from, with a link back to YouTube. It doesn't host or play any videos.

Fair use of the service

  • Don't use scripts or bots to send lookups, and don't try to get around the hourly limit.
  • Don't use TitleSift to collect comments or other YouTube data in bulk.
  • Don't send feedback that is abusive, illegal or someone else's private information.
  • Only add names you believe are right. Names you add can be shown to other visitors, and we may remove any name without notice.
